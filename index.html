Total$1,000$1,000

✧ Explanation

Cash is debited because the business received $1,000 in cash. Sales Revenue is credited because the business earned $1,000 from the sale.

A little understanding goes a long way.

Inventory / Cost of Goods Sold

If the bicycle was held in inventory, a second entry would also normally be required to record the cost of the bicycle. Since the bicycle’s original inventory cost was not provided, the entry would be:

Additional inventory entry
AccountDebitCredit
Cost of Goods Sold[Cost of Bicycle]
Inventory[Cost of Bicycle]
ⓘ

Important The debit and credit amounts must always balance.
